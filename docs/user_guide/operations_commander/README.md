## Your Role in RTSA

As an **Operations Commander** or **Watch Officer**, you are the primary decision-maker in the RTSA system. You use the Common Operating Picture (COP) to:

- Maintain real-time awareness of all entities in your operational area
- Detect and respond to anomalous or threatening behaviour
- Confirm or reject AI-generated anomaly assessments
- Direct other operators and coordinate force posture

RTSA is designed to support your judgment — it surfaces the most relevant information quickly and flags anomalies so you can focus where attention is needed most.

---

## Your Quick-Start Checklist

When you sit down at an RTSA workstation:

- [ ] Verify the classification banner colour is as expected
- [ ] Check the **Connection** indicator — is it green (live)?
- [ ] Note the number of **active alerts** in the bottom status bar
- [ ] Review the **CRITICAL and ELEVATED alerts** in the alert panel first
- [ ] Scan the **map** for RED (Hostile) and ORANGE (Suspect) tracks
- [ ] Check the **last update time** on any tracks you are tracking closely

## Important Reminders

> ⚠️ **You are the decision authority.** RTSA provides AI-scored recommendations, not orders. You decide how to respond to any alert or track assessment.

> 🔐 **Your feedback matters.** Every time you confirm or reject an AI detection, you are helping the system learn. Accurate, timely feedback improves detection quality for everyone.

> 📋 **Everything is logged.** All your interactions — including alert acknowledgments and feedback submissions — are recorded in the immutable audit trail. This is required by security policy.
